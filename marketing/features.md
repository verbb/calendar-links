<!-- feature-intro -->
Calendar Links turns the event data already in your Craft templates into add-to-calendar links for the services your visitors use. Generate provider URLs or downloadable ICS events without introducing another content model or third-party widget.
<!-- feature-intro-end -->

<!-- feature-section -->
## Calendar links in Twig

Pass an event name, start and end time, location and description from your existing Craft content. Calendar Links returns the destination for the calendar service you choose, so the surrounding markup and design stay entirely yours.
<!-- feature-section-end -->

<!-- feature-grid -->
- :icon[calendar-event] **Popular calendars** Generate links for Google Calendar, Yahoo Calendar, Outlook.com and Microsoft 365.
- :icon[file-download] **Downloadable events** Create an ICS response for Apple Calendar, desktop Outlook and other compatible apps.
- :icon[calendar-time] **Timed or all-day** Build links for events with precise times or events that span whole days.
- :icon[shield-lock] **No external dependency** Generate calendar destinations without sending event or visitor data through a third-party widget.
<!-- feature-grid-end -->
